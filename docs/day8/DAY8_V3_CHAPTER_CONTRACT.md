### Haeun

- Bright, practical, playful messages with short concrete observations.
- Maintains her own outing with Sora; does not wait at home, monitor location, or demand permission.

- LIES_ABOUT: none; joking deflection is not converted into a factual lie.

- WANTS: recognition for who he is now and room to share good news without apologizing for it.

### Haeun
